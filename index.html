Outro Swap · Chrome extension

Privacy policy

Outro Swap plays your own song over a show's ending. Your music stays on your computer. The only thing ever sent anywhere is when an episode's outro starts.

Your songs and settings

The audio files you add, your series rules and your settings are stored only in your own browser. They are never uploaded.

Shared outro timings

When Share outro timings is on (it is on by default), Outro Swap sends one small report when it finds where an episode's outro starts:

  • the streaming service's numeric episode ID
  • how many seconds before the end the outro starts, and the episode's length
  • how the time was found: the service's credits marker, the on-screen credits cue, or your own "Mark outro start"
{ "episode_id": "82132228", "from_end": 88, "duration": 1440, "source": "cue" }

That is the whole report. No name, email, account, viewing history, song names or audio is ever sent.

Reports are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. They are used only to start the outro song at the right moment for everyone using Outro Swap. Individual reports can't be read back by anyone using the extension; it only receives the middle value for an episode. Timings are never sold or shared for any other purpose.

Turning it off

Open the Outro Swap dashboard and switch off Settings → Share outro timings. With it off, nothing is sent and nothing is looked up.

Permissions

storage and unlimitedStorage keep your settings and songs in your browser. Access to the streaming site lets Outro Swap read the playback time and lower the episode's audio during your song. Access to the Outro Swap database is used only for the timings described above.
